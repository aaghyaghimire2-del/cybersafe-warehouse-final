CyberSafe Warehouse
Practical safety training, built for the floor.

HOW TO PLAY

  • MOVE — W A S D to walk around the warehouse floor, full 360° mouse-look.
  • INSPECT — aim the reticle at something and click to take a closer look.
  • JUDGE — decide for yourself: flag it as a concern, or call it fine. You'll find out right away if you were right.
  • OFFICER — click the IT officer near the entrance for general hints.
  • SITE MAP — press M any time to see the floor plan. On Level 1 (Trainee) it also pins every real issue by area; on Levels 2 and 3 it shows the layout only.
  • GOAL — correctly flag every real security issue before time runs out to clear the mission and unlock the next one.
  • SCORING — right calls earn points and XP, whether it's a real issue or something that's actually fine. Wrong calls cost points.
